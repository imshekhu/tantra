Privacy policy

Updated 12 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Tantra has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, favourites, mood check-ins and listening time are stored only on your iPhone and in its own backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Pro purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers App Store purchases.

Children

Tantra is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here and in the app, with a new date.
