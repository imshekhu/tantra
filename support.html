I can't hear the binaural beat

Binaural beats need headphones, with each ear hearing its own tone. Use the headphone check in the You tab. Turn off Mono Audio (Settings › Accessibility › Audio & Visual) and set Spatialize Stereo to Off in Control Center.

Restoring Pro on a new iPhone

Open the You tab and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancelling a subscription

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Tantra. Or tap Manage subscription in the You tab.

Listening safely

Don't listen while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. If you have epilepsy or a history of seizures, ask your doctor before using rhythmic sounds or beats. Keep the volume low.

Tantra is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.
